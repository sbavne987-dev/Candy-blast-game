❔ How to Play

Swap two nearby candies and match 3 or more. Match 4 in a straight line to create a Chocolate Stripe that clears a row or column. Match 5 in a straight line to create a Choco Sprinkle that clears candies of one type. Make a T or L shape with 5 or more candies to create a Choco Wrapper that clears the surrounding area.
